How to buy

I make display blades and cosplay props by hand. There's no checkout button. Every piece is sold person to person, to adults only, after an ID check. Here's how it works.

Seen something you want? Everything I've made is in the shop. New pieces also go up on X and Instagram.

The short version

  • You must be 18 or over. No exceptions.
  • I check your ID before you pay, and again when you get it.
  • It goes into your hands. Not a neighbour's, not a doorstep, not a locker.
  • UK only for now.
  • These are display pieces. They are not tools and they are not toys.

What I make

Display pieces and cosplay props, forged by hand from mild steel. They're made to sit on a stand or a shelf and look good. They are not built to be used.

Each one is made by hand, so no two are the same.

Going to a con? Steel won't get past the door. Ask me about a con-safe copy to carry instead. It costs extra.

The five steps

  1. Message me

    Tell me which piece you want. Discord is quickest:

    yanderebait_

    A DM on X or Instagram works too.

  2. Jump on a quick video call

    Two minutes. I need to see that you're a real person, and that the ID you're about to send is yours. Have your passport or UK driving licence in your hand.

  3. Send your ID

    A clear photo or scan of your passport or UK driving licence, plus a current photo of you.

    If you're under 18, or you'd rather not send ID, I can't sell to you. No hard feelings.

  4. Pay

    Once the check is done, I'll confirm the price and how to pay.

  5. Get your blade

    Pick one:

    • Collect it from me in London. Bring the same ID.
    • I deliver it myself, if you're within 20 miles of me. Same ID at the door.
    • An ID-checked 18+ delivery service. The driver checks your ID and hands it to you only.

    Whichever you pick, it goes to you and nobody else. Not a housemate, not a neighbour, not a safe place.

What happens to your ID

I keep the copy for 12 months, as proof that I checked. Then I delete it.

It's used for the age check and nothing else. I don't share it with anyone, unless the police or Trading Standards have a legal right to ask for it.

Once it's yours

Take it home wrapped up, and keep it at home. Carrying a blade in public without a good reason is against the law in the UK, and that includes a display piece.

I can say no

I can turn down any sale. If something feels off, I won't sell, and I don't have to explain why.
